Inflation picture

Headline inflation eased to 15.39% in August 2026 (NBS). But the CBN’s September household survey found 77.2% of households still perceive inflation as high, up from 67.2% in August — official easing hasn’t reached the market yet.

What it means for stocks

  • Rate cuts push T-bill and bond yields down, making dividend and growth stocks relatively more attractive.
  • Banks earn less on government securities when rates fall, but cheaper credit can grow loan books.
  • Consumer names benefit as easing inflation restores purchasing power.
  • The naira matters too: the street rate sat near ₦1,415/$ in early October — check the FX converter.
