T-bills vs stocks

The 364-day T-bill now yields 15.85% against August inflation of 15.39%. The risk-free return barely beats inflation. Falling T-bill yields (as now) usually make equities look more attractive, since the ‘safe’ alternative pays less. Rising yields do the opposite.
