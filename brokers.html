Before you choose

  • Ask for the exact commission rate in writing: “negotiable” means it moves.
  • Check you get a CSCS statement and can track dividends in your own name.
  • Test the app during a trading session, not just the demo.
  • Ask how corporate actions (bonuses, rights) are handled and what they cost.
