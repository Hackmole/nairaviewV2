Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an IPO and a listing by introduction?

An IPO sells new shares to the public to raise capital. A listing by introduction simply lists existing shares for trading. No new money is raised, and the “offer price” is the price the shares are introduced at.

Why do newly listed stocks often jump on day one?

First-day pops usually reflect pent-up demand meeting limited supply. Both Transcorp Power and Aradel closed about 10% above their listing prices on day one, then traded on fundamentals afterwards.
