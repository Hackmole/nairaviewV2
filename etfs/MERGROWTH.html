Costs to know

Three layers. First, the fund's own management fee, taken quietly out of the NAV. The exact figure is on the manager's factsheet, and it differs by fund. Second, your broker's commission each time you buy or sell, set by the NGX fee schedule. Third, the premium or discount to NAV at the moment you trade: overpaying here is the cost most beginners never notice.

Risks in plain English

Manager risk: you are betting on stock picks, not on the market. Active funds also tend to charge higher fees than index trackers, which is a hurdle the picks must clear.
